Plenty of offices and schools still have perfectly good monitors with DVI sockets, while modern laptops and PCs use HDMI. This BENFEI DVI to HDMI cable connects the two, so you can keep using older screens instead of replacing them. It works in both directions, so it also connects a desktop with a DVI graphics card to an HDMI monitor or TV.

It handles Full HD 1080p, and up to 1920×1200 on office monitors, in either mirror mode (the same picture on both screens) or extended mode (a second desktop for multitasking). The connectors are gold-plated, the cable is double shielded against interference, and it comes in lengths from 0.9 m to 4.5 m.

Two things to know. DVI doesn't carry sound, so audio stays on the computer's own speakers or headphones. And it's a Full HD cable, not a 4K one. For everyday office screens and classroom setups it's a simple, very inexpensive way to get an older monitor working again.
