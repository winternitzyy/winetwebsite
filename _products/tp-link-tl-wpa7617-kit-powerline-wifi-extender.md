If your Wi-Fi drops out upstairs, in a back bedroom or in a garden office, this is what we'd fit before trying a Wi-Fi extender. The TP-Link TL-WPA7617 KIT is a powerline adapter with Wi-Fi built in. It sends your internet through the electrical wiring already in your walls, then creates fresh, strong Wi-Fi in the room that needs it.

That's the key difference from a normal Wi-Fi extender. An extender has to pick up a weak wireless signal and repeat it, losing speed and stability with every wall. Powerline carries the connection through the wiring instead, so the AC1200 dual-band Wi-Fi it creates at the other end is far more dependable for video calls, streaming and homework. Each unit also has a Gigabit Ethernet port, so you can plug in a smart TV, games console or desktop PC directly, and two network cables come in the box.

Setup is plug and play: plug the small adapter in by your router and connect the cable, plug the Wi-Fi unit in where you need coverage, then press the Wi-Fi Clone button to copy your router's Wi-Fi name and password. Speeds depend on your home's wiring, and both units should go straight into wall sockets, not extension leads. If you only need a wired connection in the far room, the cheaper [TP-Link TL-PA7017P KIT](/products/tp-link-tl-pa7017p-kit-powerline-adapter/) does that job; choose this kit when you want Wi-Fi there as well.
