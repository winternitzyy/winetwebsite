When a room has weak Wi-Fi, most people reach for a Wi-Fi extender. We usually recommend a powerline adapter instead. The TP-Link TL-PA7017P KIT uses the electrical wiring already in your walls to give you a **wired** connection, and wired connections are simply more reliable than repeating a wireless signal through walls and floors.

Setting it up takes minutes. Plug one adapter in near your router and connect it with a network cable, then plug the second one in wherever you need internet: a home office, a bedroom with a games console, or a TV at the far end of the house. It's plug and play, with no software or settings. Each adapter has a Gigabit Ethernet port, supports the HomePlug AV2 (AV1000) standard, and has a passthrough socket so you don't lose the plug.

Be realistic about speed: the 1000 Mbps on the box is a theoretical maximum, and what you get depends on your home's wiring. Plug the adapters straight into wall sockets rather than extension leads for the best results. For a steady connection for video calls, streaming and gaming, it's one of the simplest and most dependable upgrades you can make.
