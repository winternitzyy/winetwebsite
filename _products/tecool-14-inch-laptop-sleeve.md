A good laptop sleeve is the cheapest insurance you can buy for a laptop. The TECOOL 14-inch laptop sleeve is a simple, well-made case that we're happy to recommend to schools and offices: it protects a laptop or Chromebook on the way to work or school without adding much bulk or cost.

Inside, a soft fleece lining stops scratches and scuffs. Outside, water-resistant fabric keeps the laptop dry when it's raining, and two front pockets hold the charger, a mouse and cables, so everything stays together. A carry handle means you can use it on its own as a light bag, or slip it into a rucksack.

It fits most 14-inch laptops and Chromebooks from HP, Lenovo, Dell, Acer and ASUS, plus the 15-inch MacBook Air and 15-inch Surface Laptop. The inside measures 35 × 25 × 2.3 cm, so very thin 14-inch laptops may have a little room to spare. It's a sleeve rather than a heavily padded bag, but for everyday carrying between home, school and the office it does the job well, and it comes in seven colours and other sizes if you need them.
