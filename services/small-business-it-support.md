## IT support that fits a small business

A small business can't afford a full-time IT department, but it can't afford IT that keeps letting it down either. Winet gives you a team that knows your systems, answers when you call, and fixes things properly so the same problem doesn't come back next week.

We've been looking after IT for schools and companies across the UK for over a decade, from our base in Westcliff-on-Sea. Whether it's one laptop that won't connect or a whole office that needs new Wi-Fi, we start by listening, then get straight to the cause.

## Plain English, no mumbo jumbo

Most IT companies talk in jargon. We talk to you like a person. We explain what's wrong, what we recommend and why, and we won't sell you anything you don't need. If something doesn't need fixing, we'll tell you that too.

## Getting started

Tell us what's going on, or what you'd like your IT to do better. We'll have a straightforward chat, look at what you have now, and suggest the simplest way to make it reliable.
