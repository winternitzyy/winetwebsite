## IT that lets teachers teach

When the Wi-Fi drops or the board won't connect, a lesson can be lost. Schools need IT support that understands the timetable, responds quickly and fixes the real cause, so the same problem doesn't come back next week.

Winet has been looking after IT for schools and organisations for over a decade. We work with school business managers and heads to keep classrooms, offices and staff connected, and we explain everything in plain English.

## Safe, reliable and sensible

Pupils need to be safe online and staff need systems they can rely on. We put in filtering, security and backups that are right for your school, without overcomplicating things or selling you what you don't need.

## Talk to us

Tell us what's working and what isn't. We'll have a straightforward conversation, look at your current setup, and suggest a clear plan to make your school's IT reliable.
